# Brand Naming Research — SportsLedger

**Research date:** 2026-03-24
**Task:** Evaluate "SportsLedger" and alternatives for the youth sports expense tracking venture

---

## Evaluation Criteria

1. **Clarity** — Does it immediately communicate "sports + money tracking"?
2. **Memorability** — Easy to say, spell, remember
3. **Domain availability** — .com preferred
4. **SEO potential** — Keywords parents would search
5. **Emotional tone** — Empowering, not anxiety-inducing
6. **Trademark risk** — No conflicts with major brands
7. **Scalability** — Room to grow beyond just expense tracking

| # | Name | Domain | Why |
|---|------|--------|-----|

## Recommendation

**Keep "SportsLedger" as the primary name.** Here's why:

1. ✅ **Domain available** — sportsledger.com is clean and unregistered
2. ✅ **SEO keywords** — "sports" and "ledger" are both search-relevant
3. ✅ **Professional tone** — "Ledger" implies trustworthy financial tracking
4. ✅ **Scalable** — works for expense tracking, benchmarking, forecasting, and beyond
5. ✅ **Zero rebrand cost** — already our working name across all docs and the GitHub repo
6. ✅ **No trademark conflicts** — no major product called SportsLedger in app stores or web

**Runner-up:** SeasonSpend — if Matt wants something warmer/less corporate.

**Action needed:** Matt to pick from the Tier 1 shortlist, then we register the domain immediately. Given the competitor signal (multiple related domains registered in late 2025), don't wait.

---

## Trademark Considerations

- "SportsLedger" — No conflicts found in USPTO TESS for Class 9 (software) or Class 42 (SaaS)
- "Ledger" is a common word — low risk of conflict (Ledger the crypto company is in a different space)
- "Sports" is generic/descriptive — cannot be trademarked alone
- Recommend filing a basic trademark application after launch once we validate ($350 filing fee)
