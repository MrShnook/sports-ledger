## TAM (Total Addressable Market)

**All US families with children in organized youth sports**

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| US children ages 6-17 | ~50 million | Census |
| Youth sports participation rate | 55.4% (2023) | Aspen Institute |
| Youth sports participants | ~27.3 million | Aspen Institute |
| Estimated households with youth sports kids | ~20-22 million | Derived (1.3 kids/household avg) |
| Average annual spend (primary sport) | $1,016 | Aspen Institute 2024 |
| Total market (family spending) | $40+ billion/year | WSJ/Aspen 2025 |

**TAM (if every sports family paid $5/mo):** 20M households × $60/yr = **$1.2 billion/year**

## SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)

**US families in competitive/travel/club sports with household income >$75K**

| Metric | Value | Source/Derivation |
|--------|-------|-------------------|
| Travel/club participation rate | ~11-20% of youth sports families | Aspen Institute (11% historical, rising) |
| Estimated travel/club families | 2.2M – 4.4M households | 20M × 11-20% |
| Midpoint estimate | **3.3 million households** | Conservative midpoint |
| Income filter (>$75K) | ~70% of travel sports families | $100K+ families 2x more likely to participate |
| SAM households | **~2.3 million** | 3.3M × 70% |
| SAM at $5/mo | **$138 million/year** | 2.3M × $60/yr |

**Why this is conservative:**
- Travel/club is growing (BSN reports 72% of club families view it as "professional")
- Income gap in participation is *widening* — wealthy families over-index
- Multi-sport families (our power users) are undercounted

## Comparable Products / Benchmarks

| Product | Users | Revenue | Model | Relevance |
|---------|-------|---------|-------|-----------|
| TeamSnap | 24M+ | Acquired by Vista for undisclosed (est. $100M+) | Freemium ($10-15/mo teams) | Team-side, not family-side |
| YNAB | ~2M users | ~$100M ARR | $14.99/mo | General budgeting, our structural comp |
| GameChanger (Dick's) | Millions | Part of $14.1B Dick's | Free + subscription | Stat-tracking, not expenses |
| Splitwise | 50M+ | ~$20M ARR | Freemium | Expense splitting, similar UX patterns |

**Key insight:** No direct comp exists. Closest analogy is YNAB (general budgeting tool that some parents hack for sports) — but purpose-built for a specific, passionate, high-spending niche.

## Growth Drivers

1. **Costs rising faster than inflation** — 46% increase 2019-2024 (2x inflation), making tracking more urgent
2. **Participation recovering post-COVID** — 55.4% (2023), 65% tried at least once (2024, highest since 2012)
3. **2026 World Cup effect** — Soccer participation expected to surge
4. **Income gap widening** — Our target segment ($75K+ HHI) is growing their sports spending faster
5. **WSJ/media attention** — Front-page coverage (March 2026) = free awareness for the problem space
6. **No competitor** — Blue ocean for 12-24+ months

---

## Risk Factors

1. **YNAB or Mint could add sport-specific templates** — Low risk (they haven't in 10+ years despite demand)
2. **TeamSnap could pivot to family-side** — Medium risk (would require major product shift)
3. **Market ceiling** — If only 5% of competitive sports families adopt, that's still 115K families
4. **Seasonality** — Youth sports spending peaks Aug-Oct (back-to-school/season start) and Jan-Feb (spring season registration)
5. **Price sensitivity** — Parents already feeling squeezed; must demonstrate clear ROI vs free spreadsheet
