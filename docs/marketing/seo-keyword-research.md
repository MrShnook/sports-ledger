# SportsLedger — SEO Keyword Research

This document outlines the initial keyword strategy for SportsLedger, targeting parents of children in competitive and travel youth sports. The keywords are categorized by the user's stage of awareness.

## 1. Problem-Aware Keywords

**Targeting parents who feel the pain but are not yet looking for a specific solution. Content for this stage should be blog posts and articles that acknowledge their frustration and provide general advice.**

-   **High-Level Pain:**
    -   "youth sports expenses"
    -   "how much do youth sports cost"
    -   "is travel sports worth the money"
    -   "expensive kids sports"
    -   "youth sports financial burden"
    -   "managing sports family finances"

-   **Sport-Specific Pain:**
    -   "travel hockey costs"
    -   "ecnl soccer fees"
    -   "mls next cost"
    -   "travel baseball expenses"
    -   "competitive dance costs"
    -   "how much does gymnastics cost"

-   **Budgeting & Tracking Pain:**
    -   "youth sports budgeting"
    -   "how to budget for travel sports"
    -   "tracking sports spending"
    -   "hidden costs of youth sports"
    -   "travel tournament budget"

-   **Long-Tail/Question-Based:**
    -   "how to afford travel sports"
    -   "are youth sports tax deductible"
    -   "why are youth sports so expensive"
    -   "saving money on youth sports"

## 2. Solution-Aware Keywords

**Targeting parents who are actively looking for a tool or method to solve their problem. Content for this stage includes comparison pages, "best of" lists, and feature-focused landing pages.**

-   **App/Software Focused:**
    -   "sports expense tracker app"
    -   "youth sports budget app"
    -   "best app to track sports expenses"
    -   "family sports finance app"
    -   "kids sports financial tracker software"

-   **Comparison/Alternatives:**
    -   "ynab for sports parents"
    -   "teamsnap vs spreadsheet for expenses"
    -   "youth sports budgeting spreadsheet template"
    -   "alternatives to quicken for sports"

-   **Feature-Specific:**
    -   "app to track travel tournament costs"
    -   "track sports equipment costs app"
    -   "youth sports tax deduction tracker"
    -   "receipt scanner for sports expenses"

## 3. Product-Aware Keywords

**Targeting users who are aware of SportsLedger and are searching for it directly. This is for the homepage, pricing page, and branded content.**

-   **Branded:**
    -   "SportsLedger"
    -   "SportsLedger app"
    -   "SportsLedger pricing"
    -   "SportsLedger reviews"
    -   "SportsLedger login"

-   **Branded + Feature:**
    -   "SportsLedger expense tracker"
    -   "SportsLedger budget tool"
    -   "SportsLedger for hockey"
    -   "SportsLedger tax deductions"

-   **Branded Comparisons:**
    -   "SportsLedger vs YNAB"
    -   "SportsLedger vs spreadsheet"
    -   "SportsLedger vs TeamSnap"

## Content Strategy Implications

-   **Top of Funnel (Problem-Aware):** Create blog content around the "pain" keywords. Titles like "The True Cost of a Travel Soccer Season" or "Are Youth Sports Tax Deductible? A Parent's Guide." This will draw in organic traffic from parents who are just starting to research their problem.
-   **Middle of Funnel (Solution-Aware):** Build landing pages comparing SportsLedger to spreadsheets and generic budgeting apps. Create pages dedicated to features like "The Best Receipt Scanner for Sports Parents."
-   **Bottom of Funnel (Product-Aware):** Optimize the homepage and core product pages for branded search terms. Ensure clear calls to action for users searching directly for the solution.
