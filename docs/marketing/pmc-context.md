# SportsLedger — Product-Market-Customer (PMC) Context

## 1. Customer

### Who are they?
- **Primary ICP:** Parents aged 30-50 in dual-income, suburban households with 1-3 children aged 6-17 participating in competitive or travel-level youth sports.
- **Key Sports:** Hockey, travel baseball/softball, competitive soccer (MLS Next, ECNL), competitive dance/gymnastics. These have the highest and most complex costs.
- **Financial Profile:** Household income typically $100k-$250k. They are not "wealthy" but have discretionary income that is being consumed by sports at an alarming rate.
- **Tech Behavior:** Comfortable with modern apps. They use TeamSnap for scheduling, Venmo for team fees, and likely a generic budgeting app like YNAB, Mint, or just a spreadsheet for personal finance. They are digitally native but not necessarily tech-early-adopters.

### What is their core problem?
They have **no visibility or control** over their family's youth sports spending. The costs are fragmented across credit card statements, Venmo transactions, club portals, and cash withdrawals, making it nearly impossible to see the "true cost" of a season.

### What are their pains?
- **Sticker Shock & Guilt:** A feeling of dread when looking at credit card statements, followed by guilt or anxiety about the total cost.
- **Decision Paralysis:** Inability to answer the question, "Can we really afford this next season?" or "Should we switch to a cheaper club?" because they don't have the data.
- **Marital Stress:** Disagreements with their spouse about the escalating costs and lack of a clear budget.
- **Time Sink:** Wasting hours manually combing through statements to build a spreadsheet, which is quickly abandoned.
- **Missed Opportunities:** Failing to claim potential tax deductions because records are a mess.

### What jobs are they trying to do?
- **JTBD 1 (Functional):** I need to track all sport-related expenses for each of my children to understand my total spending.
- **JTBD 2 (Functional):** I need to create and stick to a realistic budget for the upcoming season.
- **JTBD 3 (Emotional/Social):** I want to feel in control of my family's finances and make informed decisions about my kids' activities without feeling guilty or stressed.
- **JTBD 4 (Emotional/Social):** I want to provide the best opportunities for my kids without sacrificing our family's long-term financial health.

## 2. Market

### What is the market?
The Family-Side Youth Sports Financial Technology market. It's a sub-segment of the broader Personal Finance SaaS market, sitting adjacent to the Team-Side Sports Management market (dominated by TeamSnap, SportsEngine).

### Market Size & Opportunity
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** 20M+ US households with kids in sports. Assuming a $60/year subscription = **$1.2B**.
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market):** 2.3M families in high-cost competitive/travel sports who feel the pain most acutely = **$138M**.
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market, Year 1):** Target 0.1% of SAM = 2,300 users. Realistically, aiming for **250-1,000 paid users** = **$15k - $60k ARR**.

### Trends & Tailwinds
- **Rising Costs:** Youth sports costs are rising at 2x the rate of inflation (Aspen Institute). This increases the pain and the need for a solution.
- **"The Athletic Arms Race":** Increasing pressure for kids to specialize early and join expensive travel teams to "keep up."
- **Mainstream Awareness:** Recent WSJ and major news coverage on the "exploding costs of youth sports" is bringing the problem into the public consciousness.

### Competition
- **Direct Competitors:** None exist. This is a blue ocean. We've seen some domain registrations, suggesting others are exploring the idea, but no launched product.
- **Indirect Competitors:**
    - **Spreadsheets:** The primary solution. **Our main job is to be 10x better than a spreadsheet.**
    - **YNAB/Mint:** General budgeting tools. Lack sport-specific categories and features. High friction to set up for this specific use case.
    - **TeamSnap/SportsEngine:** Team-side platforms. They are a *source* of expenses, not a tool for managing them from the family's perspective. They are potential integration partners, not competitors.

## 3. Product

### What is the core value proposition?
**SportsLedger gives your family financial clarity and control over youth sports spending.** Stop guessing, start planning.

### What is the MVP?
A web app that does one thing perfectly: **manual expense tracking for youth sports.**
1.  **Onboarding:** Create a family, add a child, select their sport(s).
2.  **Expense Entry:** A simple form to add an expense with fields for: Amount, Date, Category (pre-filled, sport-specific list), Child, and a Notes section.
3.  **Dashboard:** A clear view of `Total Spend` for the season, a breakdown by category (e.g., Fees, Travel, Equipment), and a breakdown by child.
4.  **CSV Export:** A simple way to get your data out.

### What makes it 10x better than a spreadsheet?
- **Pre-built categories:** No setup required. We've done the thinking for them.
- **Mobile-first:** Easy to enter an expense from your phone right after you make a purchase.
- **Multi-child/sport roll-ups:** Automatically aggregates data in ways that are painful in a spreadsheet.
- **The promise of the future:** The MVP is the foundation for high-value features like receipt scanning, benchmarking, and forecasting, which are impossible with a spreadsheet.

### Positioning
- **For:** Parents of kids in competitive youth sports who are worried about the cost.
- **Who feel:** Stressed, overwhelmed, and in the dark about their spending.
- **Our product is:** A purpose-built financial tracker.
- **That provides:** A clear, complete picture of their sports-related expenses.
- **Unlike:** Generic budgeting apps or manual spreadsheets.
- **Our solution:** Gives them the data they need to make informed decisions, create budgets that stick, and feel in control of their family's financial future.
